RYEO Archive v13.9 display/admin patch

Changes
- Public record body now preserves original paragraph structure.
- Consecutive lines beginning with "- " render as real bullet lists.
- Inline attachment tokens remain supported at their original positions.
- Admin record list sorts by RY record year descending, then serial descending.
- Admin content textarea readability improved; plain-text line breaks remain the storage format.
- No database schema migration is required for these changes.

Important
- Existing records must actually contain newline characters in records.content for old data to show paragraph breaks.
- New records can be entered as plain text with blank lines and "- " bullets. No HTML/Markdown storage is required.
